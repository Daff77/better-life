Greek-Yogurt Berry Parfait

How to cook

  1. 1

    Wash the berries (if frozen, let them soften
    slightly for 2–3 minutes). Cut strawberries if
    they are large.

  2. 2

    Take a serving glass. Add ⅓ of the yogurt to
    the bottom of the glass.

  3. 3

    Sprinkle half of the berries on top of
    the yogurt.

  4. 4

    Add another layer of yogurt until it covers
    the berries.

  5. 5

    Top with the remaining berries and
    sprinkle chia seeds on top.

  6. 6

    Mix lightly before eating or let it sit for
    5–10 minutes to form a pudding-like texture.
